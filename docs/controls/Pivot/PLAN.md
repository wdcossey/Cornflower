# Pivot: plan

[#121](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/121). The 8.1 Pivot, PivotItem and PivotHeaderItem: controls
Avalonia doesn't have, so Cornflower's own (`Cornflower.Controls.Pivot`, `PivotItem`, and in
`Cornflower.Controls.Primitives` `PivotHeaderItem` and `PivotHeaderPanel`, rung 5), with the phone's default styles and
templates as the Wp81 theme's `ControlTheme`s, and the phone's motion, read frame by frame from the emulator, run on
`CornflowerClock`. The worked example for Tier 2's new controls; Hub (#122) takes its pieces next (README, "What Hub can
take").

  `Orientation` states, committed in [`ref/wp81/Pivot/`](../../../ref/wp81/Pivot/README.md), with their tree dumps.

## The motion, as modelled

`Cornflower.Controls.PivotMotion`, internal, on `CornflowerClock`. The phone's ScrollViewer offset is
DirectManipulation's, and its code moves the strip's and the items' translates from it; ours keeps an offset of its own
and moves the same two transforms.

| What | The phone (#128, #136) | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| The drag | The content with the finger | One for one, from the press, held to a page either way |
| Let go | A swipe goes on (200 px in 150 ms); a 100 px drag held 300 ms goes back | On if it was moving 0.3 px/ms or faster the way it went; back if as fast the other way; else on past half a page, back short of it (the threshold isn't read: owed) |
| The swipe's offset after the release | Exactly a page; 33 to 35 frames; the same shape whatever the speed | `KeySpline` 0.24,0.60 0.22,1 over 515.625 ms: 1.07 px rms of 450 against the median of 20 |
| A tap's (and a change from code's) | 500 ms; a page a header (900 px for two) | `KeySpline` 0.20,0.08 0.02,1 over 500 ms: 0.74 px rms against 6 |
| Back after a drag let go | 35 frames | `KeySpline` 0.25,0 0.20,1 over 546.875 ms: 0.12 px rms of 109, the one read |
| The strip | A straight line with the offset; a page of offset its leaving header's pitch, or going back the incoming header's | The same, page by page, by the pitches of the laid-out headers |

## The ScrollViewer

The phone's template holds a ScrollViewer in `ScrollViewerScrollBarlessTemplate`, and its offset is the motion's
offset, round an extent of 450,000 px kept centred so it can wrap. Ours holds the same ScrollViewer, in
`Wp81ScrollViewerScrollBarlessTheme` (#116), for what it does here that the motion doesn't: it clips the strip and the
items to the view, and its `Margin` is the Pivot's `Padding`. It doesn't scroll (both bars `Disabled`): Avalonia's
inertia and snap points aren't the phone's (#116's recorded deviation), the phone's curves are read and fitted above,
and the wrap needs no endless extent when the Pivot moves its two transforms itself. Decided on the user's behalf: the
reasons above; to reverse, if the ScrollViewer gains the phone's physics as a part class, the Pivot can drive its offset
and read it back instead.

## The gesture

A `GestureRecognizer` (`SidewaysSwipeGestureRecognizer`, internal, the Hub's too; `PivotSwipeGestureRecognizer` until the
Tier 2 roll-up) on the Pivot, not pointer handlers: so it takes part in
Avalonia's arbitration. An item's ScrollViewer, deeper, sees the press first and takes a drag up or down past its 5 px;
a control that asks for no gesture (Wp81's Slider and ToggleSwitch, the phone's `ManipulationMode="None"`) keeps its
drag; once the Pivot captures, nothing under it takes the finger. It starts past Avalonia's `ScrollGestureRecognizer`'s
distance (5) and only more sideways than up or down, in the ScrollViewer part. Touch and pen only, as Avalonia's
`ScrollGestureRecognizer`: a mouse taps the headers.

## Avalonia against the phone

Avalonia has no Pivot. `TabControl` has a strip of headers but no motion and no wrap; `Carousel` slides its items but
has no strip and no swipe of its own. Neither is a base: the Pivot is a `SelectingItemsControl` (Avalonia's selection
