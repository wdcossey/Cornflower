# Hub: plan

[#122](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/122). The 8.1 Hub and HubSection: controls Avalonia doesn't have,
so Cornflower's own (`Cornflower.Controls.Hub`, `HubSection`, `HubSectionHeaderClickEventArgs`,
`SectionsInViewChangedEventArgs`, rung 5), with the phone's default styles and templates as the Wp81 theme's
`ControlTheme`s, and the phone's motion, read frame by frame from the emulator, run on `CornflowerClock`. It takes
Pivot's pieces (#121, [Pivot's README](../Pivot/README.md), "What Hub can take").

## Where the values come from

- **The styles and templates**: the 8.1 Update 1 runtime's, `R:\cornflower-reference\flash-update1\xbf-json\hub.json`
  and `hubsection.json`, and the values of their keys from `themeresources.json`, which equal the SDK's `generic.xaml`
  and `themeresources.xaml`. The keys were already in `Tokens/` (#14).
- **The captures**: the catalogue's, run `20261108-1443-dev11`, `Hub` `two-sections`, dark and light, base and the three
  `BackgroundImageLoadStates`; all taken mid-entrance, so read for sizes and weights only
  ([`ref/wp81/Hub/`](../../../ref/wp81/Hub/README.md)). The section width on three profiles from the same catalogue on
  devices 10, 11 and 12 (runs `20261108-0754-dev10`, `-1443-dev11`, `-1451-dev12`).
- **At rest**: the probes' layout lines (run `20261116-0009-dev11`, after the entrance) and two frames of run
  `20261115-2059-dev11`'s recording, at the first section and at the last.
- **The motion**: #128 (#114), `-Probe hub`, run `20261115-2059-dev11`; #137 (#136), `-Probe hubimage` (1600 × 800 px,
  runs `20261116-0009-dev11` and `-0025-`), `-Probe hubimage900` (900 × 400, `-0001-`) and `-Probe hub` again (`-0007-`).
  Every frame's `RenderingTime`, the ScrollViewer's offset (and whether DirectManipulation calls it inertial), the
  header's left edge and transforms, each section's left edge and the background's parts.
  [`tools/refapp/README.md`](../../../tools/refapp/README.md), "Hub's header" and "Hub: a background image, and the end
  of the sections"; `tools/motion/pivot.py`.
- **#148's reads** ([#165](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/165), [#166](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/166),
  [#167](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/167)): `-Probe hubrelease` (runs `20261116-1714-dev11` and
  `-1751-`, the loop backwards, every frame's section edges and header), `-Probe hubentrance` (`-1721-`, `-1811-`, the
  entrance's `EntranceAnimationProjection` every frame, the states after `Loaded`), `-Probe hubstretch` (`-1720-`,
  `-1754-`, an image narrower than the screen). `tools/refapp/README.md`, "Pivot and Hub: where a release goes" and
  "Hub: the entrance"; `tools/motion/release.py` and `entrance.py`.
- **The curves** are fitted here from those logs (the fits' scripts are in the PR's scratch, not the repo; the medians
  they fit are written out in `HubReadings`).

The numbers the tests use are in `tests/Cornflower.Tests/Controls/HubReadings.cs`.

## The motion, as modelled

`Cornflower.Controls.HubMotion`, internal, on `CornflowerClock`. The phone's ScrollViewer offset is
DirectManipulation's, with the header as its secondary content at 1/18 and the background moved by the Hub's code; ours
keeps an offset of its own and moves the panel, the header and the background by their render transforms. Times are on
the emulator's 15.625 ms frames, frame 1 the first after the release.

## The ScrollViewer

The phone's template holds a ScrollViewer in `ScrollViewerScrollBarlessTemplate`, and its offset is DirectManipulation's,
with mandatory single snap points at the sections (every swipe ends on a section's start to 0.001 px) and the loop
past its end. Ours holds the same ScrollViewer, in `Wp81ScrollViewerScrollBarlessTheme` (#116), and doesn't scroll it
(both bars `Disabled`): the Hub moves its panel by a render transform, as the Pivot moves its items (#121). Avalonia's
inertia and snap points aren't the phone's (#116's recorded deviation), the reads give the phone's curves, and the loop
needs the sections to go past the end, which a ScrollViewer's extent stops. Decided on the user's behalf; to reverse,
scroll `PART_ScrollViewer` with `HorizontalSnapPointsType` `MandatorySingle` and read its offset in `ApplyMotion`, and
drop the loop or give the panel a copy of the first section.

## The gesture

`SidewaysSwipeGestureRecognizer`, internal, Pivot's too (one recogniser since the Tier 2 roll-up; it was
`HubPanGestureRecognizer`, a copy of Pivot's `PivotSwipeGestureRecognizer`): a `GestureRecognizer` on the Hub, in the ScrollViewer part, starting past Avalonia's `ScrollGestureRecognizer` distance (5) and only more sideways
than up or down, touch and pen only. A section's ScrollViewer keeps a drag up or down; a Slider or ToggleSwitch keeps
its drag (`PhoneNoManipulation`). A press on moving sections holds them where they are (not in the loop's last part,
where only the header moves); let go without a drag, or taken by a list in a section, they go to the nearer start.

## Avalonia against the phone

Avalonia has no Hub. A horizontal `ScrollViewer` of panels pans but has no header parallax, no image parallax, no snap
to sections on the phone's curves and no loop; `Carousel` pages a whole view at a time. The Hub is a `TemplatedControl`
with a list of `HubSection`s (WinRT's is a `Control` with `Sections`, not an `ItemsControl`), with the phone's template
and motion. Rung 5.

## Extensions

None. `Hub.Motion`, `SectionStarts`, `SectionsLength`, `ViewWidth`, `CanPan` and `HubSection.BuiltContent` are internal,
for the tests.
